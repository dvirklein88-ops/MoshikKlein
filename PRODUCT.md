# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Parents of boys approaching bar mitzvah (about age 11–13), mostly in Modiin–Maccabim–Re'ut and anywhere in Israel via Zoom. They are choosing a teacher, often months before the date, and want reassurance that their son will arrive at the day confident, without pressure, in a way that respects their home (including secular families).

## Product Purpose
A one-page landing site for Moshik Klein, a private bar mitzvah teacher. Success = a parent makes contact (WhatsApp, phone or the contact form). Core message: "הכי טוב שנדבר על זה".

## Positioning
A single, personal teacher with 20+ years of experience who connects kids to Torah naturally — warm, no pressure, no preaching ("בלי לחץ ובלי דרשות") — and adapts to the family's home and the child's pace.
