# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Parents of boys approaching bar mitzvah (about age 11–13), mostly in Modiin–Maccabim–Re'ut and anywhere in Israel via Zoom. They are choosing a teacher, often months before the date, and want reassurance that their son will arrive at the day confident, without pressure, in a way that respects their home (including secular families).

## Product Purpose
A one-page landing site for Moshik Klein, a private bar mitzvah teacher. Success = a parent makes contact (WhatsApp, phone or the contact form). Core message: "הכי טוב שנדבר על זה".

## Positioning
A single, personal teacher with 20+ years of experience who connects kids to Torah naturally — warm, at the child's pace ("בקצב של הילד") — and adapts to the family's home and the child's pace.

- Services: קריאה בתורה (נוסח אשכנזי בלבד), הפטרה, הכנה להנחת תפילין.
- Tracks: הפרשה כולה + הפטרה (start ~6 months ahead); מפטיר + הפטרה (start ~3 months ahead).
- Frontal in Modiin–Maccabim–Re'ut; Zoom from anywhere. Teaches secular families too.
- Contact: 054-4447068 (tel/WhatsApp use 972544447068), moshikklein@gmail.com. Netlify Forms contact form (name, phone required; date/parasha, message optional; honeypot).
- Static HTML/CSS/JS, published from `public/` on Netlify. Hebrew, RTL.
- Price: 500 ILS/month (125 per lesson, one lesson a week), shown in the FAQ. Undecided: domain, intro lesson (not offered on site).
